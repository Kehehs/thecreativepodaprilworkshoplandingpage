The Experience

What Unfolds

Every moment of the 90 minutes is designed with intention. This is not a class where you are taught. It is a space where you discover.

0 – 15 min
☕
Arrival & The First Question
Tea in hand, you settle in. A small folded card waits at your place with one question — not about art, but about you. One word. No pressure. The morning begins here.
15 – 25 min
✋
Ink Your Hand
You press your paint-covered hand onto paper and lift it slowly. What you see is already yours. Already beautiful. The artwork begins — without a single brushstroke of instruction.
25 – 65 min
🎨
The Creation
Forty uninterrupted minutes with warm watercolours and a theme that matters: Something My Hands Remember. No right answer. No wrong mark. Just your hands in conversation with memory.
65 – 80 min
🪑
The Circle
Everyone holds up what they made. Each person completes one sentence: My hands have known… You will hear things in this circle that you will remember long after the morning ends.
80 – 90 min
📖
The Closing
You open your arrival card. You write one word for how you feel now. Most people notice the difference immediately — in their own handwriting. Then you take your artwork home.
